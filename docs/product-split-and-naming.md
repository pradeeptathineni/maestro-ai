# Product split and naming gate

## Product decision

Search and Corpus are the permanent center of this repository.

- **Search** conducts a live, bounded investigation for a user's current need and organizes
  attributed leads.
- **Corpus** exposes durable, admitted, refreshable knowledge. It is not raw search history.

The same model-led research protocol interprets and organizes both. Their acquisition boundary,
lifecycle, and interface remain visibly different. Future orchestration or execution is a separate
product decision and is not part of this phase.

## Naming result

`Signals AI` is useful as a working description but is **not cleared for a public repository,
package, domain, or product rename**. Current active collisions include:

- [SignalsAI engineering intelligence and AI gateway](https://orgsignals.com/about), which overlaps
  directly with software-development users;
- [Revvity Signals AI](https://revvitysignals.com/products/artificial-intelligence/signals-one-signals-ai),
  which includes AI-assisted research and semantic search;
- [Signals AI trading signals](https://signalsai.app/about);
- a current [SignalsAI Visual Studio Marketplace extension](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=SignalsAI.signals-ai).

This is a practical collision assessment, not trademark counsel. Because the overlap is material,
the repository/package remain `maestro-ai` and the public rename gate is stopped pending a human
naming decision. Implementation continues under the working product concept.

Candidate naming directions for human exploration—not availability claims—are:

- Evidence Atlas
- Signal Loom
- Landscape Index
- Provenance Atlas
- Project Beacon

Any selected direction needs fresh trademark, package, repository, domain, and social-handle checks
before public use.
